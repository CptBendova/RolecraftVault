# Community conduct

Rolecraft Vault's public repository exists for respectful product feedback,
auditing, and focused collaboration.

Be constructive, patient, and specific. Do not harass others, publish personal
information, expose private vault content, share credentials or signing material,
or post working vulnerability details publicly. Disagreement about design or
implementation is welcome when it remains about the work.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and restrict participation
when needed to protect users or keep the project useful. Sensitive security
concerns belong in the private process described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
